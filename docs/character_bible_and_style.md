# Character bible and visual style guide

## Madam Lim
70, Singaporean Chinese, independent, warm, financially careful; familiar with WhatsApp and mobile banking but sometimes uncertain about new interfaces. She drinks tea at her dining table. Modest hopes: treating family to dinner, supporting a grandchild, keeping a retirement buffer. No luxury fantasy, no ridicule, no exaggerated frailty, no implication that loss is deserved.

Her face, bob, spectacles, watch, phone and home are fixed in `prompts/characters.json`. Day 1 peach blouse; Day 3 pale blue blouse; Day 4 blue blouse and sage cardigan. Scenes 10–12 safe timeline and final return to Day 1. The rewind must retain each source take’s wardrobe. Fake David is only a voice, warm then insistent. Real David is a distinct voice from the same generation. Narrator is calm and reassuring.

## Home and camera
Singapore HDB interior: cream walls, barred window at right, pale green sofa left, oak table, orchid, steel kettle, blue cup. Neighbourhood sounds are soft and specific. Show recognisable lived-in details without making a caricature of Singapore. Eye level, stable 35mm framing, 85mm face inserts; no melodramatic Dutch angles. Phone inserts use a locked mount for reversible motion.

## Continuity workflow
Approve one neutral face/home anchor and one empty room anchor. Generate scene stills with both references, then animate only approved stills. Reject face/hand/wardrobe/layout drift. Keep IDs and seeds where provider supports them, but a seed is not an identity guarantee. First-call replay uses the very same source take. Avoid speaking frontal mouth closeups; phone voices are offscreen, Lim’s brief lines use side/back framing unless reviewed lip sync is added later. Split screen pairs matching compositions. Consent-cleared fictional synthetic voices only.

## Colour and typography
Warm Day 1; natural Day 3; cool contrast and lower exposure as pressure grows on Day 4. Family vision warmer/soft, never extravagant. Loss quiet and desaturated. Safe timeline returns warm. Do not change furniture to convey mood. 16:9 1080p, 24fps, high bitrate H.264 and AAC, no flashes. White subtitle letters 44px equivalent at 1080p with dark outline/box, two lines maximum, about 42 characters per line. Cards 48px or larger with 96px margins, navy background and cream/white type. Stable educational overlays, not text generated by AI. Mandarin can use a CJK font and cue ID translation; retain speaker and timing and review line widths.

## Audio and dignity
Phone and messaging tones are original generic sounds. Music stays below voice. Loss ends in TWO seconds of digital silence. At rewind arrival all sound cuts; there are TWO silent seconds, then the narrator, without a music bed. Reversed FX are isolated from dialogue, never reversed full conversation as intelligible scam instruction. No scream, no jump scare, no excessive crying. Financial UI has no real bank logo, account number, payment destination or credentials. Onscreen transfers are educational illustrations.
